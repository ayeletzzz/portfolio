About

I studied fine arts at Bezalel before pivoting into product design — not because I stopped caring about craft, but because I wanted to use it to solve harder problems. Five years ago I joined Fullpath, an automotive AI startup of 60 people, and I've been designing there ever since — agents, CRMs, data products, the whole surface. Somewhere along the way I started shipping the CSS too.

In my free time I illustrate 🖊️, grow herbs, houseplants, and a garden I'm mildly obsessed with 🌿, and spend a suspicious amount of time thinking about coffee ☕ and wine 🍷. I love camping, and a quiet morning alone at a museum is my idea of a perfect day. I'm also a mom to a very busy toddler. Best design constraint I've ever worked with.
